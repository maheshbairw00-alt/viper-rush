Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceRefund Policy

Diamonds are digital items that are delivered to your account immediately after payment.

When we refund

  • Payment taken but diamonds not received: if your payment was successful and the diamonds did not arrive, email us with your payment ID. We will add the diamonds, or refund the full amount if we cannot.
  • Double payment: if you were charged twice for the same purchase by mistake, we refund the extra payment.
  • Payment failed but money deducted: the bank or Razorpay normally returns this money automatically within 5 to 7 working days.

When we do not refund

Once diamonds have been added to your account they are used up as virtual items and are not refundable. This includes diamonds you have already spent on spins, skins, memberships or gifts, and accounts banned for breaking the rules.

How to ask

Email Maheshbairwa00@gmail.com from the email address you use to log in. Write your player name and payment ID (it looks like pay_xxxxxxxx and is in the Razorpay receipt or SMS). We reply within 2 working days.

Time to receive a refund

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and normally reach you within 5 to 7 working days.

Cancellation

Because delivery is instant, a purchase cannot be cancelled after payment. Before paying you can close the payment window at any time and nothing is charged.
