Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceRefund Policy

Viper Rush is an online snake battle game. This page explains what information we use and why.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name, email address and profile picture, when you log in with Google.
  • Your game progress: level, coins, diamonds, items you own, friends, match results and settings.
  • Records of diamond purchases (pack, amount, date, payment ID).
  • Reports you send us about other players.

What we do NOT collect

We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN, bank details or passwords. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay, a licensed payment company. Please read the Razorpay privacy policy for how they treat payment information.

How we use it

To run your account, save your progress, show you on friend lists and match results, give you the diamonds you paid for, prevent cheating and abuse, and help you if something goes wrong.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). It is not sold to anyone. We share it only with Google (hosting and login) and Razorpay (payments), or when the law requires it.

Voice chat

If you use team voice chat, audio goes directly between the players in your team. We do not record it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing us from the address you use to log in. We will reply within 7 days.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and above. Players under 18 should buy diamonds only with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
